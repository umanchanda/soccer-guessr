SoccerGuessr

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Soccer Guessr (www.soccerguessr.com) is a daily soccer guessing game. This page explains what we collect when you play and what we do with it. In short: only what's needed to keep your scores, and we never sell it or share it for advertising.

Playing as a guest

If you play without an account, your results and in-progress rounds are stored in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us.

If you create an account

We store your email address, a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself), and your score for each round you finish. When you sign in, any results saved in your browser as a guest are added to your account.

If you sign in with Google

We receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google, and use them only to create and sign you in to your Soccer Guessr account. We don't access your contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Cookies

Signing in sets a single cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is not used for tracking or advertising. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Where your data lives

The site runs on Heroku and account data is stored in a Neon Postgres database. Match photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons, which sees your IP address when your browser loads them, as with any website.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and scores, ask us on our Discord server and we'll remove them. Guest data can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and the date above.
